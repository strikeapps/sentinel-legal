SENTINEL Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 August 2026

SENTINEL ("SENTINEL," "we," "us") is a tactical mapping and team-coordination app for search-and-rescue, public-safety, and professional field teams. This policy explains what data the app handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. Questions: privacy@strike-apps.com.

1. Data we collect

  • Email address — to create your account and sign you in.
  • Display name — the name teammates see for you in chat and on the shared map. You choose it and can change or clear it at any time.
  • Precise location — collected only while you turn on "Share my location," and used to show your position to members of your own team. Location sharing is off by default and can be switched off at any time, including in the background.
  • User content — chat messages, notes, markers, and operational data (e.g., landing-zone or MEDEVAC information) you create. This content is end-to-end encrypted (see §4) and shared only with the team you send it to.
  • Account identifiers — a server-side account ID and device install ID used to route your team's messages.

We do not collect health/PHI data, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, or any device tracking identifiers.

2. How we use data

Data is used solely to provide app functionality: authentication, delivering messages and position updates to your team, and supporting the app's tools. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

3. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We use no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

4. End-to-end encryption

Chat messages, position updates, and operational data are encrypted on your device and only decrypted on your teammates' devices, using standard cryptography (ChaCha20-Poly1305 with X25519 key agreement). Our relay server moves this content as opaque ciphertext and cannot read your messages or positions. All network traffic additionally uses TLS. Encryption keys are stored in the device Keychain and never leave your device.

5. Third parties

We rely on a small number of service providers strictly to operate the app:

  • Apple — App Store distribution and subscription billing (we never receive your payment card details).
  • Our hosting/relay infrastructure — operated for us to route encrypted team traffic; it does not have access to decrypted content.
  • Transactional email provider — to send account-verification and password-reset emails.

6. Data retention & deletion

You can permanently delete your account from inside the app (Account tool → Delete Account). Deletion removes your profile and group memberships from our server. You can also stop location sharing at any time without deleting your account.

7. Security

We protect data with end-to-end encryption of content, TLS in transit, and Keychain storage of keys and credentials on your device.

8. Children

SENTINEL is a professional tool and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be posted here with a revised date.
